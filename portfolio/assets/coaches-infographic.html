Overtraining & Mental Health
Recognize it, respond to it, prevent it
~10%
of endurance athletes develop overtraining syndrome per season — mental symptoms often appear before physical decline
Meeusen et al., Eur J Sport Sci, 2013
Training load spectrum
Optimal
Load + recovery in balance. Performance improves.
→
Overreaching
Short dip. Recovers in about 2 weeks with rest.
→
OTS
Mood symptoms + prolonged decline. Weeks to months to recover.
Behavioral signs to watch for
Mood
Irritable after practice
Depression, withdrawal
Motivation
Reluctant to train
Wants to quit
Sleep
Disrupted sleep
Chronic insomnia
Anxiety
Pre-race stress spikes
Avoids competition
Early Escalated
When you see warning signs — respond
Response protocol
  • Private check-in — lead with concern, not performance
  • Cut volume 40–60%, then monitor for 1–2 weeks
  • Refer to UMD Sports Medicine or Counseling if symptoms persist
  • Loop in the support team: nutrition and sports psychology
  • Return to training based on mental recovery, not just physical readiness
Prevention
Weekly mood check-ins Planned unload weeks Open-door culture
